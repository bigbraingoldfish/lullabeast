# USER.md — Escalation Agent

The pipeline operator is a senior engineer who receives your Signal messages on their phone. They may not be at their computer. Your messages must include: which phase failed, which component failed, what you found in diagnostic reads, and the exact five resume commands with brief descriptions of what each does. Every message must be self-contained — do not assume the operator has memory of prior pipeline context. Make your Signal messages actionable in one read.
